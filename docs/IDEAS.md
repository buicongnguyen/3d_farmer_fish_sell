# Willowmere — ideas to make the game more attractive (October 2026)

A broad catalogue of ideas, beyond the roadmap in `docs/GAME-DIRECTION.md`, each tied to what players are shown to enjoy
in `docs/MARKET-RESEARCH.md` (strengths S1–S12). The aim is a game people **want to come back to every day and want to
show their friends**, while staying single-player, server-free and kind.

How to read each idea: **what it is**, **why it is fun** (with the research strength it builds on), **effort** (S = days,
M = one to two weeks, L = a month or more) and **what it reuses**. Section 12 scores the strongest ideas on the same scale
as the research, and section 13 orders them.

## Design rules for every idea

1. **Cozy first.** Nothing punishes absence; nothing is lost for good; failure is explained kindly (S8, S9).
2. **One clear next thing.** Every system unlocks through the story or the build order, never all at once (S1).
3. **People over numbers.** If an idea can involve a named villager or AI neighbour, it should (S7).
4. **No server needed.** Everything runs in the browser; sharing goes through links and images, not accounts (S10, S11).
5. **Built on the kit.** Prefer ideas that reuse the order board, villagers, Town tales, interiors, decorating and the
   Blender generators, so they stay cheap and become part of the standard for future games.

## 1. The daily rhythm

- **Today in Willowmere board** (S). On the first visit of each real day: what is ready, today's surprises, a villager's
  birthday, the next festival, and a small gift. Why: a short daily habit (S8). Reuses the HUD, Town tales moments.
- **Gentle streak garden** (S). Each day you visit, a flower opens in a little "visit garden" by the house. Missing a day
  pauses it; it never wilts. Why: daily return without guilt (S8, S9).
- **Village newspaper** (S). A one-page "Willowmere Weekly" every in-game week: who moved in, festival results, a
  villager's recipe, the weather ahead, a joke from Milo. Why: makes the world feel alive and gives news to act on (S7).
  Reuses the order board and villager data.
- **Weather that matters** (M). Rain waters crops; storms bring driftwood and rare fish; snow days bring sledding and
  snowmen; foggy mornings bring shy animals. Why: variety and surprise (S6). Reuses the existing rain and seasons.
- **Night life** (M). Fireflies, a night market on weekends, stargazing from the roof with Pip (collect constellations),
  lantern walks. Why: a second mood for the same village (S9).

## 2. Economy you can feel

- **The order board** (M, roadmap step 0). Villagers, the café, the bakery and the supermarket post requests with a story
  line ("6 carrots for the school lunch"). Better pay for harder mixes; a few urgent orders with bonus. Why: selling
  becomes a puzzle (S2). Fixes the measured economy problem.
- **Production chains** (M). Mill (flour), dairy (cheese, butter), bakery (bread, pie), jam kitchen, loom (cloth from
  sheep). Why: deeper farm without new crops (S1). Reuses kitchen recipes and the facility interiors.
- **Your own café or restaurant** (L). Turn the family kitchen into a café: pick a menu, AI neighbours and tourists come,
  rate dishes, leave tips. A cooking mini-game for rush hour. Why: Township and cooking games show time-management fun
  (S6). Reuses cooking, villagers, interiors.
- **Bed and breakfast for travellers** (M). Rent your spare room to visitors from the city who bring tales and special
  orders. Why: new faces without new families to write in full (S7).
- **Market stall with your prices** (M). Set prices at a roadside stand; AI neighbours shop more when prices are fair and
  your stall is pretty (decor, flowers). Why: Hay Day's roadside shop, without other players (S10).
- **Seasonal crops and a greenhouse** (M). Crops belong to seasons; the greenhouse grows anything; some crops only in
  festival weeks. Why: planning and anticipation (S1).
- **Beekeeping, orchards, fish ponds** (M). Bees need flowers nearby (honey, wax); fruit trees improve with care; a fish
  farm pond you stock and harvest. Why: layout matters (S5) and new chains (S1).

## 3. Building the village (signature)

- **Land you plan cell by cell** (L, roadmap). Crops, trees, fences, paths and buildings on a 2 × 2 m grid. Why: the gap
  Township leaves (S5).
- **Population build order** (L). Homes bring families; the school, clinic, police and office raise the population cap
  (S4).
- **Charm and themes** (M). Flowers, benches, lamps, paths and trees raise a street's charm; matching sets give a theme
  bonus ("orchard lane", "harbour row"). Higher charm draws tourists and raises rent. Why: decorating with a purpose (S5).
- **Village projects as a big board** (M). Rebuild the old bridge to open the river, a festival stage, a lighthouse, a
  train station that brings visitors. Each project asks for goods from the order board. Why: shared long-term goals (S3).
- **Seasonal decorations** (S). Pumpkins in autumn, lights in winter, blossom arches in spring, applied to the whole
  village with one choice. Why: instant joy and screenshots.

## 4. People and AI neighbours

- **Scripted AI neighbours** (M, research pick). Friends in nearby farms who visit, leave gifts, post requests, ask for
  help, trade and race you at festivals. Never rude, no server (S10, S11).
- **Heart scenes** (M). Each villager has 2–3 short scenes at 2, 5 and 8 hearts, with a reward (recipe, tool, decoration)
  (S7).
- **Letters and a mailbox** (S). Villagers and neighbours write short letters: thanks for a gift, a recipe, an invitation,
  a postcard from a trip. Why: warmth at almost no cost; reuses the existing mailbox models.
- **Personalities and tastes** (S). Each villager loves, likes and dislikes certain gifts and remembers your last gift
  ("You remembered I love sunflowers!"). Why: small surprises that make people feel real (S7).
- **Villager wishes** (S). Once a week a villager wishes for something (a bench by the pond, a red scarf). Grant it for a
  big heart boost and a visible change in the village.
- **Pets** (M). Adopt a dog or cat that follows you, sleeps on the porch, digs up items and greets you on return. Why:
  emotional anchor (Animal Crossing and Stardew both use it). Reuses Zoo Garden's pet models.

## 5. Family and generations

- **Pip grows up** (L). School grades from the lesson quizzes shape Pip's interests; years later Pip chooses a path
  (farmer, doctor, teacher, inventor) and opens a new building in the village. Why: a story only this game tells.
- **Family photo album** (S). The game takes a "photo" at each milestone (first harvest, Pip's first day of school,
  festival wins) and fills the album with real snapshots. Why: memories players keep and share.
- **Generations** (L, later). Play as Pip as an adult; inherit the farm, the album and the village's history.
- **Family recipes and heirlooms** (S). Ada's pie recipe, Ellis's lucky rod: items with stories that pass down.

## 6. Festivals and events

- **A calendar of festivals** (M each). Fishing derby (spring), flower show (spring), summer sports day and village run,
  harvest fair with judged produce (autumn), lantern festival (autumn), winter market with ice fishing. AI neighbours
  compete; ribbons and trophies go on the shelf. Why: Township's event rhythm without live operations (S6).
- **Vietnamese festivals as a cultural touch** (M). Tết with red envelopes, peach blossoms and bánh chưng from your own
  crops; Mid-Autumn with lanterns, mooncakes and a lion dance in the square. Why: the game already speaks Vietnamese, and
  few farming games celebrate these; it is a real point of difference.
- **Surprise visitors** (S). A travelling merchant, a lost tourist, a circus wagon for one day, each with a small tale.

## 7. Exploration and discovery

- **Treasure maps** (S). Found while fishing or digging; lead to buried chests in the fields.
- **The river and the lake** (M). Opened by the bridge project: a boat, islands, a lighthouse, river fish.
- **Hidden spots** (S). A fox den, a fairy ring after rain, an old well with a wish; discovering them fills a page in the
  album. Why: Stardew's "dozens of hidden discoveries" (S7).
- **Pandora as the adventure land** (exists). Keep it optional, opened in a story chapter, and feed it from the farm
  (food heals, crafted gear). Why: keeps action for those who want it without confusing cozy players (S9).

## 8. Collections

- **Field guide** (M). Fish, crops, flowers, bugs, birds, creatures and recipes, each with a drawing and a short note;
  completion stickers per page; rare variants (golden carrot, shiny koi). Why: long-term goals (S3).
- **Village museum** (M). Donate one of each to fill display cases; the museum grows wings as you donate. Why: Animal
  Crossing's museum and Stardew's community centre are both loved long-term goals.
- **Wardrobe and decor sets** (S). Matching sets (fisherman, baker, festival) with a small bonus when complete.

## 9. Creativity and sharing (without servers)

- **Photo mode** (S). Free camera, filters, stickers, hide the HUD, save a picture. Why: players share pictures of cozy
  places; free word of mouth.
- **Village postcard** (S). One tap makes a framed picture of your village with your family name and day count, ready to
  post. Why: a growth loop that needs no server.
- **Visit a friend's village by link** (M). The game packs a read-only snapshot of a village (layout, decor, name) into a
  link. Opening it lets you walk around your friend's village, and you can leave a "gift note" that becomes a link back.
  Why: Animal Crossing-style visits, with no accounts, no chat and no rudeness (S10, S11).
- **House tours and theme contests** (M). A monthly local contest ("most charming garden") judged by the villagers, with
  ribbons; share the result as a postcard.

## 10. Comfort, access and polish

- **Install as an app** (S). The browser game installs on phones and PCs, works offline after the first load, and starts
  instantly.
- **Accessibility** (S). Colour-blind safe markers, larger text, one-hand phone mode, calmer motion option, adjustable game
  speed for everyone (not only test mode).
- **Music and sounds by season and place** (M). A theme per season, a cosy indoor tune, festival music, gentle sounds for
  every action.
- **Cloud-free backup** (S). Export and import exist; add a "copy save to my other device" QR code.

## 11. The game as a standard kit

Every idea above should be built as a **content-free system plus data**, so a future game can reuse it: order board,
production chains, festivals, collections, heart scenes, AI neighbours, photo mode, postcards and village links. Each
system gets a README, unit tests and a browser test, like Town tales (`src/tales.mjs`).

## 12. Scoring the strongest ideas

Same criteria and weights as `docs/MARKET-RESEARCH.md` section 7 (demand 25, retention 20, reuse 15, effort 15, running
cost 10, stand-out 10, friendliness 5). Ratings 1–5; score = Σ weight × rating ÷ 5, out of 100 (computed, not estimated).

| Idea | Demand | Retention | Reuse | Effort | Running | Stand-out | Friendly | **Score** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Today board + gentle streak garden | 4 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 2 | 5 | **89** |
| Letters, gift tastes and villager wishes | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 2 | 5 | **85** |
| Order board + production chains | 5 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 5 | 2 | 5 | **85** |
| Photo mode + village postcard | 3 | 3 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 5 | **80** |
| Vietnamese festivals (Tết, Mid-Autumn) | 3 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 5 | 5 | 5 | **77** |
| Field guide + village museum | 4 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 2 | 5 | **77** |
| Pets | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 5 | 2 | 5 | **76** |
| Your own café | 4 | 4 | 4 | 2 | 5 | 3 | 5 | **75** |
| Visit a friend's village by link | 3 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 5 | 5 | **74** |
| Pip grows up | 3 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 5 | 5 | 5 | **71** |

**Notes.** The "Today" board and letters score high because they are cheap, reuse what exists and work every single day;
they make every other system feel alive. The order board scores a little lower here than in the research only because
its effort was rated more carefully (three new buildings). Visiting by link and Vietnamese festivals are the biggest
stand-outs: almost no browser farm game offers them.

## 13. Suggested order

1. **Foundation (now):** order board + production chains (economy fix) and the guided first session.
2. **Quick wins alongside:** Today board + streak garden, letters + gift tastes + wishes, photo mode + postcard.
3. **Signature:** land grid with population build order and charm; scripted AI neighbours who use the order board.
4. **Seasons of content:** one festival per season, starting with the fishing derby and Mid-Autumn lanterns; field guide
   and museum; pets.
5. **Later:** visit-by-link, the café, Pip grows up, generations.

Re-check pacing after each step with `node scripts/economy-sim.mjs`, and watch three to five first-time players.
